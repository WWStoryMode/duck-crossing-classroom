Outcome A · Recognise

Identify when a pedestrian signal permits crossing to begin.

Possible mechanic: press when the signal turns green.

Outcome B · Evaluate

Judge when crossing is safe given signals and approaching traffic.

Possible mechanic: observe traffic and decide whether to cross or wait.
